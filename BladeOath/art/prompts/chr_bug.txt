Style lock (original dark xianxia 'ink and indigo' pixel set): 16-bit pixel art for a top-down 3/4 view action game. TRUE LOW-RESOLUTION pixel art: every art pixel is a large crisp square block of equal size, chunky clean clusters, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur, no dithering noise. 1-pixel dark indigo outline (#161a26), never pure black. Muted low-saturation palette: ink greys (#22262e, #353a44, #4c525c) and cold steel (#b8c2cc, #7c8894, #4e5866); light from the upper-left, 3 shades per material. Gold, bright red and white are RESERVED for gameplay effects: never use gold, bright red or large white areas. No large areas of pure black. No coins, no dice, no religious symbols, no talismans or paper charms, no bagua, no Taoist robes, no temples, no incense burners, no deities, no halos. Original design only, no existing franchise or game. Background: perfectly flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF chroma-key filling the whole canvas, no checkerboard, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no text, no letters, no numbers, no grid lines, no frames.

Enemy 'gu bug': a fat poisonous beetle-like insect, segmented moss-green carapace (#8fb069, #5f7d48, #3b5030) with ink-grey plates, short pincer mandibles.

Layout: exactly 2 rows on the pure magenta #FF00FF background, wide empty gaps between every item, no two items touch.
Row 1 (reference): the full character three times, standing, same scale: FRONT view, SIDE view facing RIGHT, BACK view.
Row 2 (cutout parts for a skeletal rig, each part drawn separately and centred in its own space, same scale as row 1): (1) carapace body seen from FRONT (head towards viewer), (2) carapace body SIDE view facing right, (3) carapace body seen from BACK, (4) the pincer mandibles alone, horizontal, pointing RIGHT. The body parts have NO legs (legs are drawn by the game).
Each part has its own 1-pixel outline all round and nothing attached to it. The bug is about 12 art pixels tall, so every art pixel is a big visible square.
