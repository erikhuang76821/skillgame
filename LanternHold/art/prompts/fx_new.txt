Style lock (LanternHold pixel set, shared by every asset): original 16-bit pixel art for a top-down 3/4 view night-forest tower-defense game. TRUE LOW-RESOLUTION pixel art: every art pixel is a large crisp square block of equal size, chunky clean clusters, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur, no dithering noise. 1-pixel dark outline in deep indigo (#1b1530), never pure black. Small shared palette: deep indigo and teal night tones, warm lamp orange #ffb64a as the key light, light comes from the upper-left. Enemies are cool violet shadow creatures with a bright lilac rim highlight (#c6a8ff); allies are warm readable silhouettes in amber, brown and teal (#6fd3c4). No large areas of pure black or pure white. No coins, no religious symbols, no deities. Original design only, no existing franchise or game. Background: perfectly flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF chroma-key filling the whole canvas, no checkerboard, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no text, no letters, no numbers, no grid lines, no frames.

An effects sprite sheet, 3 rows x 4 frames, every frame separate with wide empty gaps, each frame about 16x16 art pixels, each art pixel a big visible square, frames left to right are an animation: row 1 poison cloud (a toxic green bubbling gas cloud puffing up, peaking, bubbles popping, fading); row 2 aura pulse (a warm golden-orange flat ground ring seen from above at 3/4, expanding and fading, a plain ring, not a halo, not a coin); row 3 summon dust (pale bone-grey dust and small bone fragments rising from the ground in a swirl, growing then dissipating).
