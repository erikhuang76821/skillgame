Style lock (LanternHold pixel set, shared by every asset): original 16-bit pixel art for a top-down 3/4 view night-forest tower-defense game. TRUE LOW-RESOLUTION pixel art: every art pixel is a large crisp square block of equal size, chunky clean clusters, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur, no dithering noise. 1-pixel dark outline in deep indigo (#1b1530), never pure black. Small shared palette: deep indigo and teal night tones, warm lamp orange #ffb64a as the key light, light comes from the upper-left. No large areas of pure black or pure white. No coins, no religious symbols, no deities. Original design only, no existing franchise or game. Background: perfectly flat solid pure green #00FF00 chroma-key filling the whole canvas, no checkerboard, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no text, no letters, no numbers, no grid lines, no frames.

Each row is one animation, frames ordered left to right. Every frame shows the SAME character at the identical scale, facing RIGHT (side view as seen from a 3/4 top-down camera), feet on one shared baseline per row. Frames are evenly spaced with wide empty green gaps; no two frames touch. The character is about 16x18 art pixels, so each art pixel is a big visible square.
